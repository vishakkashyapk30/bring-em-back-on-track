## Rules to verify before submitting

- ☑ Every decision shown to an agent has a plain-English reason
- ☑ A human can review anything that affects a customer
- ☑ No protected attributes in decisions (constraint tests green; fairness report: ☑ proxy and outcome tests on the promise-break model, ☑ NBA action rates by group, 1 group (separated) sent to review)
- ☑ Financial hardship is flagged (hardship latch visible in the UI)
- ☐ Benchmark CSV produced by `jobs benchmark` from the final build, not hand-edited
- ☐ Final commit before 21:00 IST; tag `submission`
- ☑ Judges' live questions: Ask tested on unseen phrasings, not just benchmark wording
