# Collections Agent FAQ (FAQ-COLL-001)

**Q1. How long does the reduced-payment programme last?**
The reduced-payment programme lasts 6 months.

**Q2. Does the National Do Not Call List stop collection calls?**
No. It applies to telemarketing only.

**Q3. Can I call a customer who filed a consumer proposal?**
No. All collection contact stops on the filing date.

**Q4. What is the grace period on a promise to pay?**
Two business days after the promised date.
