# Mid-stage Collections Call Script (SCR-MID-STD-EN, v2.0)

1. Greet, give your name and Maple Bank, and read the recording notice: "This call may be recorded for quality
   and training purposes."
2. Confirm you are speaking with the customer and verify identity (date of birth) **before** disclosing any debt.
3. State the purpose and the past-due amount accurately.
4. Listen. If hardship is mentioned, follow POL-COLL-004 §4.2 before asking for payment.
5. Agree a promise to pay or an arrangement; read back the amount and date.
6. Close respectfully and confirm next steps.
