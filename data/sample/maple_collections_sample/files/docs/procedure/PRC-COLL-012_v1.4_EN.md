# Dispute Handling Procedure (PRC-COLL-012, v1.4)

## §6.1 Raising a dispute
If a customer disputes a fee, a transaction, or says a payment was not applied, the agent opens a dispute and
pauses payment requests for the disputed amount until it is resolved. Fraud claims are transferred to the
fraud team.

## §6.2 Late-fee disputes after incidents
Fees charged because of a bank system incident are reversed without the customer having to prove payment.
See IR-2026-0914.
