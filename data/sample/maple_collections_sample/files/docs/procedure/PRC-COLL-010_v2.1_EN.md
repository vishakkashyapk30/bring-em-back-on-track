# Promise-to-Pay Procedure (PRC-COLL-010, v2.1)

## §3.1 Taking a promise
A promise to pay (PTP) records an amount and a date the customer commits to. The due date should be within
**21 days** and, where possible, on or after the customer's next payday.

## §3.2 Confirming the promise
The agent must read back the amount and date before ending the call, and offer a payment link. A reminder is
scheduled before the due date.

## §3.3 Kept and broken promises
A promise is **kept** when the promised amount is received by the due date plus a grace period of
**2 business days**, **partially kept** when some but not all of it is received, and **broken** when nothing is
received. Promise-kept rate = kept / (kept + partially kept + broken), excluding open and cancelled promises.
