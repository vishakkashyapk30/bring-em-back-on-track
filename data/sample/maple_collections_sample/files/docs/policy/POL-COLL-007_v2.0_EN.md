# Complaints Handling in Collections (POL-COLL-007, v2.0)

## §8.1 Recording a complaint
Any expression of dissatisfaction is a complaint. Record it the same day and escalate to a team lead.

## §8.2 Escalation stages
Frontline (5 business days) -> bank ombudsman -> external complaints body. Inform the customer of each stage.
