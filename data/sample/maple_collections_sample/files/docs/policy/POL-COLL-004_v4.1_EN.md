# Collections Hardship and Vulnerable Customers Policy (POL-COLL-004, v4.1) — SUPERSEDED

Effective 2025-03-01. Superseded by v4.2 on 2026-03-01.

## §4.2 Recognising hardship and offering options
When a customer states a hardship, the agent should consider offering a programme. The reduced payment
programme reduces the payment by 40% for 2 months. Income evidence is required for every programme.

## §4.5 Vulnerable customers
Vulnerable customers should be transferred to a team lead.
