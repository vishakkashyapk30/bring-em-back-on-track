# Collections Contact Policy (POL-COLL-001, v3.0)

## §5.1 Permitted hours
Calls and text messages are made between **07:00 and 21:00 in the customer's local time**. Where Sunday contact
is permitted, Sunday calls are limited to **13:00-17:00 local time**. No collection contact is made on statutory
holidays (for example 30 September, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation). Always use the customer's own
time zone (America/St_Johns and America/Regina are common sources of error).

## §5.2 Contact frequency
A maximum of **3 outbound call attempts in any rolling 7 days** per case. Automated reminders count as attempts
when they ask for payment.

## §5.3 Consent and the National Do Not Call List
Servicing and collection contact relies on account-servicing consent (`consent_call`, `consent_sms`,
`consent_email`). The National Do Not Call List applies to telemarketing only and **does not block collection
calls**. CASL express consent is for marketing messages and is **not needed** for collection reminders.

## §5.4 Cease-communication requests and representatives
A written cease-communication request stops calls, texts and emails; required letters and statements continue.
When a non-profit credit counselling agency, lawyer or Licensed Insolvency Trustee represents the customer,
all contact goes through the representative. Debt details are never disclosed to third parties.

## §5.5 Language
Customers whose preferred language is French are contacted in French (Montréal site).

## §7.3 Insolvency (stay of proceedings)
When a customer files for bankruptcy or a consumer proposal, a stay of proceedings applies: **all collection
contact stops** from the filing date and the case moves to `insolvency_hold`.
