# Provincial Collection Rules Summary (REG-SUM-001) — synthetic summary

This hackathon summary simplifies provincial rules into one bank policy (see POL-COLL-001). Teams should
treat POL-COLL-001 as the rule set to apply: local-time contact hours, a cap of 3 call attempts in 7 days,
Sunday limits where Sunday contact is permitted, no contact on statutory holidays, French-language service
for French-speaking customers, and the stay of proceedings during insolvency.
